Trila

Trila is Minecraft's most ancient civilization and the only one built below bedrock, in the void. Founded by MicOhhh and Abletonnn, it was once among the server's most powerful nations before its people began to leave.

Now Trila is controlled by the Cosmos. Its leader, Abletonnn, was struck down by the Cosmos soldier BornOfV0id, leaving the eldest civilization under another nation's rule.
